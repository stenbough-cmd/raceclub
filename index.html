What Is Race Club

Race Club is a persistent online league built on Le Mans Ultimate racing software. It's not a lobby you drop into for one race and forget. It's real racing with real people, using real WEC and ELMS entries on real racetracks around the world.

Every round you run, every result you post, and every season you complete carries into the next. You're building an actual sim racing career, one race at a time.

The League Hub is where that career lives. Standings, results, and news update the moment a round is scored. Follow your own progress or scout the whole grid. Every race lives here.

Because Race Club is built on a WEC-style format, the season isn't wall-to-wall sprint races. Endurance rounds are mixed into the calendar too, and they reward a different kind of driver. Those who can manage tires and fuel, and know when to push and when to hold back, will find themselves fighting for wins.

Race Club is for everyone, not just those who post the fastest lap.

Keep scrolling to see everything we have to offer. When you're done, sign up for a profile and join the fun!

The Experience

This Isn't Just A Lobby. It's A Sim Racing Career.

Every system underneath Race Club exists to make one thing feel real: The seat you're in and the season you're racing are actually yours.

Real Teams, Real Seats

Every car on the grid is modeled on an actual real-world WEC team. Join the team that fits how you want to race.

Race Any Class You Want

The moment a season is open for registration, choose your preferred class. Start in an LMGTE or jump in a Hypercar. The choice is yours.

A Real Governing Body

A Steward Board rules on filed protests using rules adapted from real FIA/WEC driving standards.

WEC-Style Scoring

Points tables modeled on the real WEC season, with race lengths that actually vary round to round.

A Fresh Grid, Every Season

Seats reset when a season ends, but your career record carries forward. Join a new team and write the next chapter.

Every Race, Tracked

Results are pulled straight from your actual league race data. Standings, points, and your driving record update the moment a round is scored.

Before You Join

What You'll Need

Race Club runs on Le Mans Ultimate sim racing software.

Le Mans Ultimate

Every race happens in LMU itself. You'll need your own copy of the game and a PC that can run it.

A Wheel Or Controller

Bring whatever setup you already race with. A wheel and pedals are recommended, but not required to join.

Discord

Not required to race, but strongly encouraged. It's where the Race Club community actually lives.

The Ladder

Class Tiers

Race Club features five classes ranging from LMGTE up to Hypercar. Classes are open the moment the league puts it on the schedule.

LMGTE

The entry point to Race Club. The most forgiving cars on the grid, built for a driver's very first race.

Rookie

LMGT3

The next step up from LMGTE. Still accessible, more competitive, and the proving ground for everything above it.

Rookie

LMP3

Considered a stepping stone to the faster LMP2 class. Still rookie-friendly but rides a finer edge.

Intermediate

LMP2

The mid-tier proving ground on the way to Hypercar. Where a driver's skill and commitment really start to show.

Hypercar

The pinnacle of a Race Club's online career. These are the fastest, most demanding cars on the roster.

Race Club Championship

Fancy A Single Player Career?

Race Club Championship, which is currently in development, is our offline mode: Take on a full season against AI rivals in the same classes, teams and points system as the league. Pick your seat, race each round in Le Mans Ultimate, upload your results, and watch your standings, results and bonus points build round by round, all at your own pace.

Request early access to Championship mode using the Feedback form when you create your profile.
