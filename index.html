What Is Race Club

Race Club is a persistent, solo-driver career built on real Le Mans Ultimate league races. You join a real team, race real league rounds against real friends, and build a driving record that carries your career forward: no NPC grid, no shortcuts, just your results, race after race.

The Experience

This Isn't Just A Lobby. It's A Career.

Every system underneath Race Club exists to make one thing feel real: that the seat you're in and the season you're racing are actually yours.

Real Teams, Real Seats

Every car on the grid is modeled on an actual real-world team, not a made-up livery. Join the team that fits how you want to race, first-come first-served, and the seat is yours.

Race Any Class You Want

No money gate, no experience checklist. The moment the league is racing a class, it's open to you: pick the seat that actually fits how you want to race.

A Real Governing Body

A per-season Steward Board rules on filed protests using rules adapted from real FIA/WEC driving standards. Final, fair, transparent.

WEC-Style Scoring

Points tables modeled on the real WEC season, with race lengths that actually vary round to round -- not the same sprint format every week.

A Fresh Grid, Every Season

Seats reset when a season ends, but your career record carries forward. Join a new team and write the next chapter.

Every Race, Tracked

Results are pulled straight from your actual league race data, not typed in by hand. Standings, points, and your driving record update the moment a round is scored.

Before You Join

What You'll Need

Race Club runs on real simracing hardware and a real league schedule, not a browser tab.

Le Mans Ultimate

Every race happens in LMU itself. You'll need your own copy of the game and a PC that can run it.

A Wheel Or Controller

Bring whatever setup you already race with. A wheel and pedals are recommended, but not required to join.

Discord

Race Club's community, scheduling, and results all run through our Discord server.

This Is Your Legacy.

Every race, every choice, every season: it all becomes part of the record you're building.
