What Is Race Club

Race Club is a persistent, solo-driver career built on real Le Mans Ultimate league races. You buy into a team, race real league rounds against real friends, and build a bank balance and a driving record that carry your career forward: no NPC grid, no shortcuts, just your results, race after race.

The Experience

This Isn't Just A Lobby. It's A Career.

Every system underneath Race Club exists to make one thing feel real: that the seat you're in, the reputation you're building, and the money in your account are actually yours.

Real Teams, Real Seats

Every car on the grid is modeled on an actual real-world team, not a made-up livery. Buy into the team that fits how you want to race, and the seat is yours.

Reputation You Earn

No shortcuts to the top. Moving up a class takes real seat time, real attendance, and a clean record on track. Money alone won't get you there.

Every Team Comes With Sponsors

Every team is bundled with its own sponsors before you ever commit. Chase a demanding, high-payout lineup or a steady, conservative one. Either way, hit the conditions and it lands in your bank account.

A Real Governing Body

A per-season Steward Board rules on filed protests using rules adapted from real FIA/WEC driving standards. Final, fair, transparent.

Real Money On The Line

Sponsor bonuses land every race, a season-end prize pool pays out by standing, and you can wager rivals for the fun of it. Once a wager's accepted, both stakes are locked in until the results are in.

A Fresh Grid, Every Season

Seats, sponsors, and wagers all reset when a season ends, but your bank balance carries forward. Buy into a new team, chase new sponsors, and write the next chapter of your career.

This Is Your Legacy.

Every race, every choice, every season: it all becomes part of the record you're building.
