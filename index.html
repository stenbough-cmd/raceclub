What Is Race Club

Race Club is a persistent, solo-driver career built on real Le Mans Ultimate league races. You register a driver, race real league rounds against real friends, and build a career record that actually means something over time — no NPC grid, no reset button, just your results, race after race.

The Experience

This Isn't A Lobby. It's A Career.

Every system underneath Race Club exists to make one thing feel real: that the seat you're in, the reputation you're building, and the money in your account are actually yours.

Real Teams, Real Seats

Every car on the grid is an actual real-world team — not a made-up livery. One driver per seat, claimed first come, first served.

Reputation You Earn

No shortcuts to the top. The Hypercar seat is earned through real seat time and a clean record — money alone won't get you there.

Sponsors Watch How You Drive

Land sponsors that pay for the way you race — clean and consistent, or fast and fearless. Either way, it shows up in your bank account.

A Real Governing Body

A per-season Steward Board rules on disputed incidents using rules adapted from real FIA/WEC driving standards. Final, fair, transparent.

Real Money On The Line

Wager against rivals, chase bonuses, manage a real bank balance. Every commitment you make is guaranteed — nobody backs out of a debt.

A Fresh Grid, Every Season

Seats reset when a season ends. Re-sign with a new team, set new goals, and write the next chapter of your career.

This Is Your Legacy.

Every race, every choice, every season — it all becomes part of the record you're building.

The Ladder

Class Tiers

Four classes, one climb. Every driver starts somewhere real — where you end up is up to you.

Bronze

Where most careers begin. Accessible, competitive, and the proving ground for everything above it.

LMP3

Step Up

Rookie friendly but a step up. Faster cars and higher stakes means bigger swings in reputation and reward.

LMP2

Silver

The mid-tier proving ground between GT3 and Hypercar. Where a serious career really starts to show.

Gate: Money + Reputation

Hypercar

Gold

The pinnacle of a Race Club career. Earned through proven, clean conduct, not bought outright.
