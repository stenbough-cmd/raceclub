The Experience

This Isn't Just A Lobby. It's A Sim Racing Career.

Every system underneath Race Club exists to make one thing feel real: The seat you're in and the season you're racing are actually yours.

Real Teams, Real Seats

Every car on the grid is modeled on an actual real-world WEC team. Join the team that fits how you want to race.

Race Any Class You Want

The moment a season is open for registration, choose your preferred class. Start in an LMGTE or jump in a Hypercar. The choice is yours.

A Real Governing Body

A Steward Board rules on filed protests using rules adapted from real FIA/WEC driving standards.

WEC-Style Scoring

Points tables modeled on the real WEC season, with race lengths that actually vary round to round.

A Fresh Grid, Every Season

Seats reset when a season ends, but your career record carries forward. Join a new team and write the next chapter.

Every Race, Tracked

Results are pulled straight from your actual league race data. Standings, points, and your driving record update the moment a round is scored.

What You'll Need

Race Club Championship

Fancy A Single Player Career?

Race Club Championship, which is currently in development, is our "offline league" for those who can't commit to a league schedule. Take on a full season against AI rivals in the same classes, teams and points system as the league. Pick your seat, race each round in Le Mans Ultimate, upload your results, and watch your standings, results and bonus points build round by round, all at your own pace.

Create Your Season. Live The Dream.

Line up in the World Endurance Championship or the European Le Mans Series against real motorsports drivers, in the real liveries you know from the broadcast. Pick the series, build the calendar, and race it your way. It feels like living the dream of racing in one of endurance racing's biggest championships.

Race Club's story generator follows along with your season. From the moment your season is compiled until the last checkered flag, it turns your results, rivalries and swings of fortune into a compelling story you can read as the championship unfolds.

Visit the Championship page for more info. Request early access to test out Race Club Championship by using the Feedback form when you create your profile.

The Ladder

Class Tiers

Race Club features five classes ranging from LMGTE up to Hypercar. Classes are open the moment the league puts it on the schedule.

LMGTE

The entry point to Race Club. The most forgiving cars on the grid, built for a driver's very first race.

Rookie

LMGT3

The next step up from LMGTE. Still accessible, more competitive, and the proving ground for everything above it.

Rookie

LMP3

Considered a stepping stone to the faster LMP2 class. Still rookie-friendly but rides a finer edge.

Intermediate

LMP2

The mid-tier proving ground on the way to Hypercar. Where a driver's skill and commitment really start to show.

Intermediate

Hypercar

The pinnacle of a Race Club career. These are the fastest, most demanding cars on the roster.
