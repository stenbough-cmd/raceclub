What Is Race Club?

Race Club is a community-driven, league website for players of Le Mans Ultimate. Compete in the online league against other Race Club drivers or run your own offline championship against AI rivals. Your results, racing history, and reputation become part of a career that grows with every season.

Online League

Race Club's Online League

This Isn't Just A Lobby. It's A Sim Racing Career.

Every season gives you the chance to represent a different team or manufacturer, fight for a new championship amongst new rivals, and build a racing record that continues long after the final checkered flag. Race Club brings the structure and progression of a racing career to your online competition.

Real Teams, Real Seats

Choose from entries inspired by real-world WEC and ELMS teams. Claim an available seat and represent your chosen team throughout the season.

Choose Your Class

From LMGTE to Hypercar, explore the classes available in Race Club. Choose from the eligible seats when registration opens and find the challenge that suits your ambitions.

A Stewarding System

A stewarding board reviews submitted protests using driving standards adapted from real FIA and WEC regulations.

WEC-Style Scoring

Compete for championship points using scoring inspired by the real WEC format, with race lengths that vary throughout the season.

A Fresh Grid Every Season

Seats reset when a season ends, but your career record carries forward. Choose a new team and begin the next chapter.

Every Race, Tracked

When a round is scored, your results update your championship standings, points, and racing history.

Your Unique Identity

Earn The Helmet. Wear The Moment.

Unlock helmets through racing milestones and achievements. Your collection becomes part of your Race Club identity, appearing alongside your results, standings, and driver profile. Collect special editions, celebrate your accomplishments, or create your own design.

Limited Edition

Awarded to the very first drivers in Race Club. A limited edition identity that will never be re-released.

The Process

How To Join The Online League

From creating your driver profile to competing in your first championship round, getting started is straightforward.

1

Create Your Driver Profile

Sign up for Race Club and set up your driver profile. No tryouts or drafts required.

2

Claim A Team Seat

Explore the available entries and claim an eligible seat when registration opens. Seats are limited and allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

3

Race The Championship

Compete against other Race Club drivers in scheduled online rounds at circuits featured in Le Mans Ultimate.

4

Build Your Racing Record

As each round is scored, your results update your standings, points, and career history.

Race Club's Offline Championship

Your Championship. Your Calendar. Your Career.

Can't commit to a regular online league schedule? Race Club Championship brings the structure of an endurance racing season to your own single-player experience in Le Mans Ultimate.

Choose between WEC and ELMS-inspired grids, build your calendar, select your team and car, and race against AI opponents on your own schedule. Follow your results as each round shapes your championship.

Create Your Season. Live The Dream.

Experience your own version of endurance racing. Build a season around the teams, cars, and liveries you recognize, then take to the track and see how your championship unfolds.

As your season progresses, Race Club's story generator turns race results, rivalries, and dramatic swings of fortune into a developing championship narrative.

A real seat

Sign for a real team and car from the WEC or ELMS grid, and race the drivers the game puts in every other car.

Your calendar, your rules

Pick the series, year, classes, tracks, race lengths, weather and points. Race each round whenever you like.

Race Recap

Full results, a lap-by-lap race report, the race settings and every lap you drove.

Driver Report

Your race in charts: position, tyre wear, fuel, pit strategy, pace and contacts, against the whole class and your nearest rivals.

Every season saved

Finished seasons stay here with their standings, race recaps and stories. Pick any of them from the Season dropdown to look back.

Season Stories

A news story written after every round, plus a season preview and a finale, all drawn from what actually happened in your races.

Find Your Place

Class Tiers

From accessible GT racing to the fastest endurance prototypes, Race Club features five classes inspired by the world of endurance motorsport.

Each class offers a different challenge, from learning racecraft and building consistency to mastering high-performance prototypes. Explore the available classes and find the racing experience that fits your goals.

LMGTE

The entry point to Race Club. The most forgiving cars on the grid, built for a driver's very first race.

Rookie

LMGT3

The next step up from LMGTE. Still accessible, more competitive, and the proving ground for everything above it.

Rookie

LMP3

Considered a stepping stone to the faster LMP2 class. Still rookie-friendly but rides a finer edge.

Intermediate

LMP2

The mid-tier proving ground on the way to Hypercar. Where a driver's skill and commitment really start to show.

Intermediate

Hypercar

The pinnacle of a Race Club career. These are the fastest, most demanding cars on the roster.
